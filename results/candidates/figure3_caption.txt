(a) The method, one row per version. Only the placement step differs: n placements against n/K. (b) Wall clock against peak memory, one point per reference size from 402 to 7 051 sequences, both tools at 8 threads. (c) The four stages on 7 051 sequences with 25 folds, one node per shard. Only placement distributes. At four shards they finish within 0.6 s of each other; at two the spread is 26.8 s because 25 folds split 13 and 12. (d) Flags per dataset by the taxonomic distance of the injected error. Grey and hatched together are the baseline before injection, the hatched part being the baseline flags that disappear after it; above them, the injected errors found and the collateral flags on untouched sequences. The percentage is the share of collateral flags inside a clade the injection touched. Untouched sequences are not known negatives. (e) Recall against the share of a sequence's surviving relatives that already carry the wrong label, both tools. Both follow the same curve, which is why half of every injected mislabel is found by neither.
